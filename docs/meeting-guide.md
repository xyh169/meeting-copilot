# Meeting guidance

Start with the purpose of the meeting. Separate what people know, what they assume, what they propose and what they decide.

Useful follow-ups clarify a specific unknown in the current discussion: an owner, deadline, dependency, acceptance criterion, evidence gap or change of plan. Ask one natural question at a time and avoid repeating answered questions.

An answer should preserve the speaker's qualification. "Not tested yet" is an answer, not evidence of success. A proposed budget is not an approval. An action without a deadline remains incomplete. A related technical statement does not necessarily answer a question about ownership or execution.

Use exact speech as evidence, preserve corrections and later details, and distinguish a recorded claim from independent verification. Research can provide context and alternatives, but it does not rewrite what was actually said.
