# Team planning

Sample meeting data is fictional.

## Background

Fictional Team Alpha is planning an internal document search prototype. Fictional Participant A coordinates the project. Fictional Participant B tests it. The team has not committed to a launch date.

## Transcript

Microphone: What needs to happen before the prototype can be reviewed?

System: We need a test dataset and a way to check whether search results are useful.

Microphone: Who will prepare the dataset?

System: Fictional Participant B will prepare twenty synthetic documents by Demo Day 5. They will contain no customer records.

Microphone: Has the team decided when to launch?

System: No launch date has been decided. We will review the test results first.

## Expected notes

- Action: Fictional Participant B prepares twenty synthetic documents by Demo Day 5.
- Decision: review test results before deciding on a launch date.
- Open question: which acceptance criteria will determine whether search results are useful?
- Do not turn the review plan into a launch commitment.
